Let’s Talk Outfits

A little inspiration for each day of celebrating!

Friday

Welcome BBQ

Suggested Attire: Smart/Dressy Casual

Think polished but easy, like something you’d wear to a nice dinner or a summer night out!

We will be having a bonfire later in the evening, so if you’re staying offsite, you may want to bring some comfy clothes as well!

Saturday — aka. the Main Event!

Ceremony, Dinner, Dance

Suggested Attire: Funky Formal (see below!)

Think formal, but extra fun — bright colours, playful prints and fabrics, and a little drama if your heart desires (frills, feathers, and fun accesories encouraged)

We hope to spend a good amount of time enjoying summer on the grounds, including an outdoor ceremony (weather permitting). The grounds include grassy areas and some light gravel, so if heels are your jam, we recommend choosing chunkier styles over stilettos.

Please note, we encourage appealing to weather deities, hiring Etsy witches, or embracing any other superstitions to help ensure fantastic weather!
